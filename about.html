Our story

Twelve years, five chapters

20142017202020232026
01 / 05
2014

Two desks, one big idea

Stackly began in a rented room with two desks, a shared monitor and a belief that small brands deserve big thinking.

2017

The studio gets a door

Our first proper office opened. Designers, strategists and developers moved into one room, and the work got sharper overnight.

2020

Going remote-ready

We rebuilt our process for screens and shared boards, then kept the habits that made distance feel small.

2023

Design meets code

An in-house build team joined, so every identity we shipped came with a fast, animated website to match.

2026

280+ launches and counting

Clients in 18 countries and 36 awards later, we still sketch on paper first and still answer every email ourselves.

What we believe

Four values we never trade

01Curiosity

Curiosity

We ask the awkward questions first. Great brands start with understanding people, not with picking a colour.

02Craft

Craft

Every curve, kerning pair and easing curve is reviewed by hand. Details are where brands feel real.

03Candor

Candor

Honest feedback, early and kindly. No surprises in the process and no padding in the invoice.

04Courage

Courage

The safe option is rarely memorable. We push every client toward the idea that makes them a little nervous.

Our manifesto

We don't decorate, we design with a point of view. Every colour, every curve and every pixel has to earn its place, because attention is expensive and people remember what feels true.
